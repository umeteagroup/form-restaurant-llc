# California Restaurant LLC Reference

Use this as an execution reference. Verify fees, deadlines, forms, and legal status with official sources before final instructions.

## Official Checkpoints

- California Secretary of State: Articles of Organization for California LLC; Statement of Information due within 90 days and then every two years.
- IRS: EIN application after the state entity exists.
- California Franchise Tax Board: LLC annual tax and Form 3522; annual LLC return requirements.
- CDTFA: Seller's permit for taxable restaurant sales; add locations/sub-locations as needed.
- City: business license for each store city.
- County/city environmental health: food facility permit, plan check, inspections, food safety requirements.
- California ABC: alcohol license if serving beer, wine, or liquor.
- EDD/payroll: employer payroll setup when hiring.

## Formation Sequence

1. Confirm structure: one operating LLC, parent/store LLC structure, or existing entity expansion.
2. Search/confirm legal name and DBA needs.
3. Prepare and file CA SOS Articles of Organization.
4. Save filed copy and entity number.
5. Draft and sign operating agreement.
6. Adopt initial company resolutions.
7. Apply for EIN.
8. File initial Statement of Information.
9. Pay or calendar FTB annual tax and annual return obligations.
10. Register seller's permit with CDTFA.
11. Apply for city business license.
12. Apply for health department plan check/food facility permit.
13. Apply for ABC license if alcohol applies.
14. Set up payroll, workers' compensation, insurance, bookkeeping, POS, and bank account.
15. Assemble closing binder.

## Professional Review Triggers

Require attorney/CPA review if any of these apply:

- More than one owner.
- Unequal economics, vesting, buy-sell terms, investor rights, or manager deadlock terms.
- Parent company plus separate store LLCs.
- Non-U.S. owners or foreign-owned disregarded LLC.
- S-corp election, partnership special allocations, or franchise tax planning.
- Alcohol license, franchise model, trademark licensing, or securities/fundraising.
- Lease guaranty, asset purchase, or acquisition of an existing restaurant.

## Restaurant Permit Notes

For each location, track city and county separately. Silicon Valley locations may involve Santa Clara County, San Mateo County, Alameda County, or another local health department. Do not assume one county's food permit process applies to another city or county.

Health permit work often depends on lease status, plans, equipment schedule, menu, floor plan, grease interceptor, fire inspection, signage, and zoning. Capture these dependencies in the generated checklist.
