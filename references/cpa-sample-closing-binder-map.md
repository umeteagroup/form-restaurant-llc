## Entity Formation Validation Rules

Use these checks after documents are downloaded:

- Legal name must match exactly across Articles, acknowledgment, LLC-12, operating agreement, ledger, bank package, and EIN application.
- Entity number / file number must be recorded in the closing binder.
- Filing dates should be recorded separately for:
  - Articles of Organization
  - Statement of Information
  - EIN assignment
- Agent name and address should match the approved filing unless intentionally changed.
- Principal office, mailing address, and California office should be checked for consistency.
- Management structure should be checked against the operating agreement.
- If the brand differs from legal name, confirm DBA / fictitious business name workflow.
- For restaurant entities, business type should be clear enough for permits and tax registrations.

## CA SOS Approved Document Expectations

After CA SOS approval, the Work Queue / public record should eventually support these downloads or equivalent records:

- Filed Form / Articles of Organization
- Business Entity Filing Acknowledgment
- Welcome Letter
- Receipt

If the portal shows download errors, capture:

- Work Queue screenshot showing `Approved`
- Public Business Search result showing `Active`
- Available download list
- Error message

Then retry download later and do not mark the closing binder complete until official PDFs are saved.

## EIN Lessons

The sample CPA package includes an IRS CP 575 EIN assignment letter. For future packages:

- Save CP 575 immediately after EIN issuance.
- Record EIN in a controlled-access tax/banking handoff, not in broad internal templates.
- Record IRS name control if visible.
- Confirm the responsible party and tax classification with CPA, especially when a single-member LLC is owned by a corporation.

## LLC-12 Lessons

The sample Statement of Information confirms the filed LLC-12 should capture:

- Entity No.
- Principal office
- Mailing address
- California office
- Manager(s) or member(s)
- Agent for service of process
- Type of business
- Email notification preference
- CEO, if required by the form flow
- Labor judgment statement
- Electronic signature and filing date

## Internal Document Lessons

Operating agreement and ledger should not be treated as government filings, but they are core closing binder documents. For restaurant/store LLCs, confirm:

- Sole member / parent company identity
- Ownership percentage and units
- Capital contribution amount
- Management authority
- Bank and tax authorization
- Transfer restrictions
- Profit/loss allocation
